One of the world's premiere thieves, Felicia Hardy glides across the battlefield with swift precision and a dancer's grace, ever ready to purloin an opponent's fortune. Those who meet the Black Cat's gaze should beware, as her omens never miss their mark!

Felicia Hardy is one of the most notorious thieves in the world. Once the Black Cat has her eyes on a prize, it's already as good as gone. This is one feline femme fatale you don't want to cross... because luck is always on her side!

After being ousted from the Thieves Guild, the Black Cat was recruited by Wilson Fisk to infiltrate Doctor Strange's Sanctum Sanctorum. Her job: Steal the Tablet of Life and Time so the Kingpin can harness its ancient power to counter Alchemax's dangerous timestream incursion.
